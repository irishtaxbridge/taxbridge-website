Payroll Services for Employers

Accurate, compliant payroll under PAYE Modernisation, ready for the 2027 minimum wage and PRSI changes.

Updated for Budget 2027

Simplifying payroll for your business

Under PAYE Modernisation, employers must report pay and deductions to Revenue on or before every pay date. Errors and late submissions lead to penalties. We take that burden off your shoulders.

Budget 2027 update

What employers need to know for 2027

  • The national minimum wage rises by 79 cent to €14.94 per hour from 1 January 2027 (from €14.15).
  • The employer PRSI lower-rate threshold rises from €552 to €600 per week from 1 January 2027.
  • PRSI rates rose by 0.15 percentage points on 1 October 2026: employees pay 4.35%, and employers pay 9.15% (lower rate) or 11.40%. A further rise is scheduled for 1 October 2027.
  • New tax credits (€2,125), a €46,500 rate band and a wider 2% USC band (€30,300) flow through employees' January RPNs.
  • Enhanced Reporting Requirements: from 1 January 2027, tax-free payments can be reported on or before the payment date, or by the 14th of the following month.

Our payroll services include

  • Revenue Payroll Notifications (RPNs): retrieving each employee's latest tax credits and cut-off points, including the January 2027 updates.
  • Payslip processing: calculating net pay, PAYE, USC and PRSI accurately at the new 2027 rates.
  • Secure payslips: password-protected payslips emailed to your staff.
  • Revenue submissions: filing the payroll submission every pay period.
  • Monthly returns: confirming your liability and payment, due by the 23rd of the following month through ROS.
  • Minimum wage checks: making sure every rate of pay meets the new €14.94 floor from 1 January.

Employee benefits

We handle Benefit-in-Kind on company cars, health insurance and other non-cash benefits, and advise on the Small Benefit Exemption, which lets you give staff up to €1,500 a year in vouchers (up to five) tax-free.
