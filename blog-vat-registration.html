VAT Registration in Ireland 2027: Thresholds, Rates & Deadlines

Updated 12 November 2026 7 min read TaxBridge Team
Updated for Budget 2027

Budget 2027 made no change to Ireland's main VAT rates or registration thresholds. But the 9% rate for food, catering and hairdressing that began on 1 July 2026 matters for many businesses. Here is everything you need to know about VAT in 2027.

Key Takeaways

  • Register once turnover exceeds €42,500 (services) or €85,000 (goods)
  • Budget 2027 made no change to VAT rates or thresholds
  • Food, catering and hairdressing have been at 9% since 1 July 2026
  • Most businesses file bi-monthly VAT3 returns, due by the 23rd via ROS
  • Turnover under €2 million? You may use the cash receipts basis

VAT Registration Thresholds

Type of businessThreshold (12 months)
Supply of services€42,500
Supply of goods (90%+ of turnover)€85,000
Intra-EU acquisitions of goods€41,000
EU distance sales to consumers€10,000

These thresholds have applied since 1 January 2025. You must register once you exceed, or expect to exceed, the threshold.

Should You Register Voluntarily?

Registering below the threshold lets you reclaim VAT on business purchases. It often makes sense if your customers are VAT-registered businesses, or if you have large start-up costs. It usually does not suit businesses selling mainly to consumers, because it raises your prices.

Irish VAT Rates in 2027

RateExamples
23%Most goods and services, professional services, electronics, alcohol
13.5%Building services, repairs, cleaning, short-term car hire
9%Food and catering services and hairdressing (since 1 July 2026), newspapers, gas and electricity
4.8%Livestock and the farmers' flat-rate addition (up from 4.5% in 2027)
0%Most food, children's clothes and shoes, oral medicines, books, exports

How to Register for VAT

  1. Register through ROS (eRegistration). Individuals who do not use ROS can register through myAccount.
  2. Provide details of your business activity, expected turnover and your bank account.
  3. Revenue may ask for evidence of trading, such as invoices, contracts or a lease.
  4. Once approved, you receive your VAT number and filing frequency.

VAT Returns: Frequencies and Deadlines

Most businesses file a VAT3 return every two months. Returns and payment are due by the 19th of the month after the period ends, or the 23rd through ROS. Smaller businesses can apply for less frequent returns. You also file an annual Return of Trading Details (RTD).

Reclaiming VAT on Business Purchases

Once registered, you can reclaim VAT on most business purchases, as long as you hold valid VAT invoices. VAT on entertainment, most car purchases and petrol cannot be reclaimed. Diesel used for business can.
