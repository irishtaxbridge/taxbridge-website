Key Irish Tax Deadlines for 2027 You Cannot Afford to Miss

Updated 12 November 2026 6 min read TaxBridge Team
Updated for Budget 2027

Missing a Revenue deadline means surcharges, interest and stress. Here are the key Irish tax dates for 2027, including the Budget 2027 changes that come into force during the year. Where a date falls on a weekend or bank holiday, Revenue normally moves it to the next working day.

Key Takeaways

  • Employers pay PAYE, PRSI and USC by the 23rd of each month (via ROS)
  • VAT3 returns are due by the 23rd after each two-month period (via ROS)
  • The 2026 Form 11 and 2027 preliminary tax are due 31 October 2027
  • Claims for 2022 tax refunds close on 31 December 2026
  • PRSI rises again on 1 October 2027

Monthly Recurring Deadlines

Employers: PAYE, USC and PRSI deducted in a month must be paid by the 14th of the following month, or the 23rd if you pay through ROS.

Companies: your CT1 return is due by the 23rd of the ninth month after your financial year ends.

PAYE Workers: Little Deadline Pressure

If you only have PAYE income, you do not have a filing deadline. But you can only claim refunds for the last four years, so the date that matters most is 31 December, when the oldest year drops out.

What Happens if You Miss a Deadline?

  • Late filing surcharge: 5% (max €12,695) within two months, or 10% (max €63,485) after that.
  • Late payment interest: charged daily on unpaid tax.
  • Loss of the ROS extension if you file and pay late.

If you have missed a deadline, act quickly. The sooner you file, the lower the cost, and we can help you put things right.
