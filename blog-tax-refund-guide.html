PAYE Workers

How to Claim Your Tax Refund for 2023–2026 (and Your Last Chance for 2022)

Updated 12 November 2026 7 min read TaxBridge Team
Updated for Budget 2027

Revenue allows you to claim tax back for up to four years. Right now that means 2022 to 2025, and from 1 January 2027 the window moves on to cover 2023 to 2026. That makes this your last chance to claim for 2022, which closes on 31 December 2026. Many PAYE workers leave hundreds, sometimes thousands, of euro unclaimed simply because they never file a return.

Key Takeaways

  • You can claim refunds for the previous four tax years
  • 2022 claims close on 31 December 2026, so act now
  • Most PAYE workers claim through Revenue myAccount
  • Rent, medical, remote working and flat-rate expenses are the most-missed reliefs
  • Budget 2027 raises the main tax credits to €2,125 from January

Why Are So Many Refunds Unclaimed?

The PAYE system gives you your standard credits automatically, but many reliefs depend on your own expenses, such as rent, medical bills or working from home. Revenue does not know about these unless you tell it, so it cannot refund you until you file a return.

Deadline: 31 December 2026

The four-year time limit means any refund owed for 2022 is lost for good after 31 December 2026. If you rented, paid medical bills or worked from home in 2022, claim now.

What Can PAYE Workers Claim?

Medical expenses20% relief on GP, consultant, prescription, physio and non-routine dental costs

Mortgage interest reliefAvailable for the 2023 to 2026 tax years only
Home Carer creditUp to €1,950 (2026), rising to €2,050 (2027)

Step-by-Step: How to Claim

  1. Log in to Revenue myAccount at revenue.ie.
  2. Select "Review your tax" and choose the year you want to claim for.
  3. Request a Statement of Liability, or complete an income tax return (Form 12) for that year.
  4. Add your credits and expenses, such as rent, medical costs or remote working.
  5. Submit. If you have overpaid, Revenue issues a refund to your bank account.

Documents You Will Need

  • Your PPSN and myAccount login details
  • Rent details: landlord's name and address, RTB number if available, and rent paid
  • Medical receipts (Revenue may ask to see them for up to six years)
  • Utility and broadband bills if you worked from home
  • Tuition fee receipts and mortgage interest certificates, if relevant

How Long Does It Take?

Once a claim is submitted online, Revenue often processes it within a few weeks, and refunds are paid directly into your bank account. Claims covering several years can take longer.

Budget 2027 tip

From January 2027 your Personal and PAYE credits rise to €2,125 each, and the 20% rate band widens to €46,500. These apply automatically, but reliefs like rent and medical expenses still need to be claimed.
