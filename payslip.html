What the lines on your payslip mean

  • Income tax (PAYE): 20% up to your standard rate band, 40% above it, less your tax credits.
  • USC: a separate charge at 0.5%, 2%, 3% and 8%. You pay none if your income is €13,000 or less.
  • PRSI: social insurance. The employee Class A rate is 4.35% from 1 October 2026 and is due to rise to 4.5% on 1 October 2027.
  • Pension: workplace contributions reduce income tax within age limits, but not USC or PRSI. Auto-enrolment contributions do not get tax relief.

Estimate only, not tax advice. 2027 figures are provisional and subject to the Finance Bill 2026 and Social Welfare Bill; the 2027 single-parent credit is assumed to stay at €1,900. Assumes one job for a full year, PRSI Class A, under age 65, standard PAYE credits, and that any unused spouse band and credit is moved to you. Amounts are spread evenly over the year, so a payslip with a bonus or emergency tax will differ. Medical insurance relief and mortgage interest relief are not included.
