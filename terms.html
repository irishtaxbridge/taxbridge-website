1. About these terms

These terms apply to the services provided by TaxBridge ("we", "us", "our") to you ("you", "the client"), and to your use of this website. By engaging our services or using this website, you agree to these terms. For ongoing services, such as bookkeeping or payroll, we will also send you a written letter of engagement setting out the specific scope and fees. If that letter conflicts with these terms, the letter of engagement applies.

2. Our services

We provide tax and accounting services to individuals and businesses in Ireland, including:

  • Form 12 and PAYE tax reviews and refund claims
  • Form 11 self-assessment returns and preliminary tax calculations
  • Bookkeeping and management accounts
  • Payroll processing and PAYE Modernisation reporting
  • VAT registration, VAT3 returns and Returns of Trading Details
  • Business start-up advice and registrations with the CRO and Revenue

We act on the basis of Irish tax law and Revenue practice in force at the time we do the work. Tax law changes regularly, for example through the annual Budget and Finance Act, and we cannot be responsible for changes made after our advice is given.

3. Acting as your tax agent

To deal with Revenue on your behalf, you will need to authorise us as your tax agent, which links your Revenue record to our Tax Advisor Identification Number (TAIN). This lets us view your record and file returns and claims for you. You can remove this authorisation at any time by contacting us or Revenue.

Before we can act for you, Irish anti-money laundering law requires us to verify your identity. We will ask for photo ID and proof of address. We cannot start work until these checks are complete.

4. Your responsibilities

  • You must give us complete, accurate and timely information, and tell us promptly about any changes in your circumstances.
  • You remain legally responsible for your own tax affairs, including the accuracy of any return filed on your behalf, and for paying any tax due to Revenue on time.
  • You should review any return or claim we prepare before we submit it, and confirm it is correct.
  • You must keep your receipts and records for at least six years, as Revenue may ask to see them.
  • You must tell us straight away about any letter, audit notice or enquiry you receive from Revenue.

We are not responsible for penalties, surcharges or interest arising from information that was incomplete, inaccurate or provided late.

5. Fees and No Refund, No Fee

No Refund, No Fee. For retrospective PAYE tax reviews and refund claims, we only charge a fee if we secure a refund for you. If no refund is due, you pay nothing for that review.

  • We will tell you our fee, or how it is calculated, before we start work. Fees are quoted including VAT where VAT applies.
  • Revenue normally pays refunds directly into your bank account. Our fee is then payable within 14 days of you receiving the refund.
  • For other services, such as Form 11 returns, bookkeeping, payroll and VAT, we agree a fixed or recurring fee with you in advance. Invoices are payable within 14 days unless agreed otherwise.
  • If Revenue later reduces or reclaims a refund because of information you gave us, the fee remains payable on the amount you received.

Where you engage us at a distance, for example by WhatsApp, phone or email, you normally have 14 days to cancel. If you ask us to start work within that period, you may have to pay for work already done, and you lose the right to cancel once the service has been fully provided.
