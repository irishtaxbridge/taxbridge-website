VAT Services for Irish Businesses

Registration, returns and rates advice for Irish businesses, kept up to date with Budget 2027.

Updated for Budget 2027

Do you need to register for VAT?

VAT registration is mandatory once your turnover exceeds, or is likely to exceed, these thresholds in any 12-month period. Budget 2027 made no change to the thresholds, which have applied since 1 January 2025:

€42,500Supply of services
€85,000Supply of goods (90%+ of turnover)
€41,000Intra-EU acquisitions of goods
€10,000EU distance sales to consumers

You can also register voluntarily to reclaim VAT on business purchases, which often makes sense for businesses selling to other VAT-registered businesses.

Budget 2027 update

VAT in 2027: what is and isn't changing

  • No change to the standard 23% rate, the 13.5% reduced rate or the registration thresholds.
  • The 9% rate for food and catering services and hairdressing, introduced on 1 July 2026, continues.
  • The 9% rate on gas and electricity remains in place.
  • The farmers' flat-rate addition rises from 4.5% to 4.8% for 2027.
  • The VAT compensation scheme for charities increases from €10 million to €15 million.

Returns and compliance

Most businesses file a VAT3 return every two months. Payment is due by the 19th of the month after the period ends, or the 23rd if you file and pay through ROS. You also file an annual Return of Trading Details (RTD).

Our VAT services

  • Registration: handling your eRegistration through ROS to get your VAT number.
  • VAT3 returns: preparing and filing your periodic returns on time.
  • Return of Trading Details: filing the annual RTD that Revenue requires.
  • Rates advice: confirming which rate applies to your products and services, including the new 9% categories.
  • Cash receipts basis: if your turnover is under €2 million, you can pay VAT only when your customers pay you, which helps cash flow.

Figures reflect Budget 2027 as announced on 6 October 2026. Most changes apply from 1 January 2027 and some are subject to the Finance Bill 2026. We will always confirm exactly what applies to you.
