Budget 2027 update

Good news for new businesses in 2027

  • Start-up company relief is extended to 31 December 2030. It gives full corporation tax relief where the liability is under €40,000, and marginal relief up to €60,000 (linked to employer PRSI paid).
  • The EII, SCI and SURE investment reliefs are extended, subject to new EU state aid rules.
  • Capital Gains Tax falls to 31% for disposals from 7 October 2026.
  • Sole traders get a higher Earned Income Tax Credit of €2,125.
  • If you hire, the minimum wage is €14.94 per hour from 1 January 2027, and the employer PRSI lower-rate threshold rises to €600 per week.
