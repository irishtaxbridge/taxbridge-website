Rent Tax Credit 2027: Now €1,150 Per Person (€2,300 for Couples)

Updated 12 November 2026 5 min read TaxBridge Team
Updated for Budget 2027

The Rent Tax Credit helps renters in Ireland offset high accommodation costs. Budget 2027 increased it by €150 to €1,150 per person (€2,300 for a jointly assessed couple) for the 2027 and 2028 tax years. You can also claim for previous years, but claims for 2022 close on 31 December 2026.

Key Takeaways

  • Worth €1,150 per person in 2027 and 2028 (€2,300 per couple)
  • Worth €1,000 per person for 2024, 2025 and 2026
  • Claims for 2022 (€500) close on 31 December 2026
  • Parents can claim for rent they pay for a student child
  • People receiving HAP or other housing supports for the same property cannot claim

Who Qualifies for the Rent Tax Credit?

You may qualify if you:

  • Pay rent for your main home in Ireland under a qualifying tenancy
  • Pay rent for a student child attending an approved course, in qualifying accommodation
  • Pay income tax, as PAYE or self-assessed
  • Are not receiving Housing Assistance Payment (HAP), RAS or another housing support for that property
Renting from a relative?

In general, rent paid to a parent or child does not qualify. Revenue may also ask for your landlord's details and the tenancy's RTB registration number, so keep them to hand.

The credit is worth 20% of the rent you pay, up to the maximum for that year. It reduces your tax bill euro for euro, but you cannot get back more than the tax you have paid.

How to Claim the Rent Tax Credit

PAYE workers (through myAccount)

  1. Log in to Revenue myAccount.
  2. For the current year, go to "Manage your tax". For past years, go to "Review your tax".
  3. Select Rent Tax Credit and enter your landlord and tenancy details.
  4. Enter the rent you paid and submit.

Self-assessed taxpayers

Claim the credit in your Form 11 return. TaxBridge can handle this as part of your annual return.

Claiming for Previous Years

If you have rented since 2022 and never claimed, you could recoup up to €4,250 per person (€500 + €750 + €1,000 + €1,000 + €1,000 for 2022 to 2026).

Act before 31 December 2026

The 2022 credit (€500 per person) can only be claimed until 31 December 2026. After that it is gone.
