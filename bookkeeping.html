For every business in Ireland, accurate books are a legal requirement. Poor record-keeping leads to penalties, Revenue audits and missed opportunities to claim valid business expenses.

Budget 2027 update

What Budget 2027 means for your company's books

  • Corporation tax stays at 12.5% on trading income.
  • The small company preliminary tax threshold rises from €200,000 to €350,000 of prior-year liability.
  • New flexibility: preliminary tax obligations are met if you pay at least 80% of the current year's liability on time and top up within four months of year-end.
  • Start-up relief for new companies is extended to 31 December 2030.
  • The R&D tax credit is enhanced: subcontracting limits rise to 20% or €200,000, and the first-year payment threshold rises to €105,000.
  • Capital Gains Tax falls to 31% for disposals from 7 October 2026.

Good books make these reliefs easier to claim. We will flag anything that applies to you.
