Starting a Business in Ireland in 2027: Your Tax Registration Checklist

Updated 12 November 2026 7 min read TaxBridge Team
Updated for Budget 2027

Starting a business in 2027? Budget 2027 extended start-up company relief to 2030, cut Capital Gains Tax to 31% and raised the Earned Income Tax Credit to €2,125. Here is your step-by-step checklist to get set up correctly from day one.

Key Takeaways

  • Choose between sole trader and limited company first
  • Register with the CRO, then with Revenue for the right taxes
  • Start-up company relief is extended to 31 December 2030
  • VAT registration is required above €42,500 (services) or €85,000 (goods)
  • Hiring? The minimum wage is €14.94 per hour from January 2027

As a rule of thumb, a company often becomes more tax-efficient once profits are well above what you need to live on, but it depends on your circumstances. We will run the numbers with you.

The Complete Tax Registration Checklist

  1. Register your business name with the CRO if you trade under a name other than your own, or form your company.
  2. Companies: register your beneficial owners on the RBO register.
  3. Register for tax with Revenue: income tax (sole traders) or corporation tax (companies).
  4. Register for VAT if you will exceed €42,500 (services) or €85,000 (goods), or if voluntary registration suits you.
  5. Register as an employer before you pay your first employee, including yourself if you are a company director.
  6. Open a separate business bank account.

Setting Up Good Bookkeeping from Day One

Use cloud accounting software from the start, keep every receipt, and reconcile your bank account monthly. Good records make your returns cheaper and faster, and make it easy to prove expenses if Revenue asks.

Tax Reliefs for New Businesses in 2027

  • Start-up company relief: extended to 31 December 2030. Full corporation tax relief where the liability is under €40,000, and marginal relief up to €60,000, linked to employer PRSI paid.
  • SURE and EII: tax relief for founders and investors who put money into a new company. Both were extended in Budget 2027.
  • Capital Gains Tax: cut to 31% for disposals from 7 October 2026.
  • Local Enterprise Office supports: grants, mentoring and the Trading Online Voucher.
Hiring in 2027?

The minimum wage rises to €14.94 per hour from 1 January 2027. The employer PRSI lower-rate threshold rises to €600 per week, which keeps more lower-paid roles on the 9.15% employer rate.
