Form 12 Submission Service

Claim every credit and relief you are entitled to as a PAYE worker. We review the last four years and handle the claim with Revenue.

Updated for Budget 2027

What is Form 12?

Form 12 is the annual income tax return for PAYE employees and pensioners, including people with non-PAYE income of less than €5,000 a year. Most PAYE workers assume their employer has everything covered, but many credits and reliefs are only granted if you claim them, so thousands of euro go unclaimed every year.

Budget 2027 update

What changes for PAYE workers from 1 January 2027

  • Personal and Employee (PAYE) tax credits each rise by €125 to €2,125.
  • The 20% standard rate band rises by €2,500 to €46,500 for a single person (€55,500 for a married one-income couple).
  • The 2% USC band now runs up to €30,300 (up from €28,700).
  • The Rent Tax Credit rises to €1,150 per person (€2,300 for a couple) for 2027 and 2028.
  • The Home Carer Tax Credit rises by €100 to €2,050.
  • Mortgage Interest Tax Relief was not extended: 2026 is the final year you can claim it.

The Government estimates a worker on €50,000 will pay over €700 less in income tax and USC in 2027. Read our full Budget 2027 guide

Common reliefs we claim for you

  • Medical expenses: 20% tax relief on GP and consultant fees, prescriptions, physiotherapy and non-routine dental work.
  • Rent Tax Credit: €1,000 for each of 2024, 2025 and 2026, and €1,150 from 2027. Couples get double.
  • Remote working relief: 30% of your electricity, heating and broadband costs for the days you work from home.
  • Flat-rate expenses: fixed deductions for many professions, including nurses, teachers, retail staff and tradespeople.
  • Tuition fees: 20% relief on qualifying third-level fees. Budget 2027 reduces the amount that is disregarded, so more families will benefit.
  • Mortgage interest relief: still claimable for the 2023 to 2026 tax years. After 2026 the relief ends.
  • Home Carer Tax Credit: for married couples where one spouse cares for a child or dependent relative at home.

Our process

We review your taxes for the last four years. In 2027 that means 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. Our No Refund, No Fee policy applies to retrospective claims, so you have nothing to lose.

Deadline: 31 December 2026

Claims for the 2022 tax year close on 31 December 2026. After that, any refund due for 2022 is lost for good. Contact us now if you have not yet reviewed 2022.
