Updated for Budget 2027

What is Form 12?

Form 12 is the annual income tax return for PAYE employees and pensioners, including people with non-PAYE income of less than €5,000 a year. Most PAYE workers assume their employer has everything covered, but many credits and reliefs are only granted if you claim them, so thousands of euro go unclaimed every year.

What we can claim for you

If you paid for any of these between 2023 and 2026, you are probably owed money back. We claim them all for you.

Rent Tax Credit

Paying rent on your home, or your child's college accommodation? Claim up to €750 for 2023 and €1,000 for each of 2024, 2025 and 2026. Couples can claim double.

Medical & dental expenses

Get 20% back on GP and consultant fees, prescriptions, physiotherapy, hospital bills and non-routine dental work such as crowns and root canals.

Remote working relief

Worked from home? Claim 30% of your electricity, heating and broadband costs for the days you worked at home.

Flat-rate job expenses

Fixed yearly deductions for many jobs, including nurses, teachers, retail staff, healthcare workers and tradespeople.

Tuition fees

20% relief on qualifying college and third-level tuition fees, for yourself or someone you paid for.

Home Carer Tax Credit

For married couples or civil partners where one partner cares for a child or dependent relative at home.

Budget 2027 update

What changes for PAYE workers from 1 January 2027

  • Personal and Employee (PAYE) tax credits each rise by €125 to €2,125.
  • The 20% standard rate band rises by €2,500 to €46,500 for a single person (€55,500 for a married one-income couple).
  • The 2% USC band now runs up to €30,300 (up from €28,700).
  • The Rent Tax Credit rises to €1,150 per person (€2,300 for a couple) for 2027 and 2028.
  • The Home Carer Tax Credit rises by €100 to €2,050.

The Government estimates a worker on €50,000 will pay over €700 less in income tax and USC in 2027. Read our full Budget 2027 guide

Deadline: 31 December 2026

Claims for the 2022 tax year close on 31 December 2026. After that, any refund due for 2022 is lost for good. Contact us now if you have not yet reviewed 2022.

Your questions: what can I claim?

Can I claim for rent?

Yes, if you rented your home or paid rent for your child's student accommodation. The Rent Tax Credit is €750 for 2023 and €1,000 for 2024, 2025 and 2026 per person. Your landlord must be registered with the RTB, or the accommodation must be approved student housing.

Can I claim for doctor, dentist and pharmacy bills?

Yes. You get 20% back on most medical costs not covered by insurance: GP and consultant visits, prescriptions, physiotherapy, hospital stays and non-routine dental work like crowns, root canals and orthodontics. Routine cleanings and fillings don't qualify.

I worked from home. What can I claim?

You can claim 30% of your electricity, heating and broadband bills, divided by the number of days you worked from home. Even a couple of days a week adds up over four years.

Is there anything I can claim just for my job?

Yes. Many jobs have a flat-rate expense that reduces your tax every year, with no receipts needed. Nurses, teachers, shop staff, healthcare workers, cleaners and tradespeople are just some examples. Tell us your job and we'll check.

Can I claim college fees?

Yes. You get 20% relief on qualifying third-level tuition fees, whether you paid for yourself, your child or another person.

Who can file a Form 12?

PAYE employees and pensioners in Ireland, including people with less than €5,000 a year of other income, such as a small rental income.

What does it cost?

Nothing up front. We only charge a fee if we get you a refund, and we tell you the fee before we file anything.
