1. Who we are

TaxBridge ("we", "us", "our") is a tax consultancy providing tax return, refund, bookkeeping, payroll, VAT and business start-up services to individuals and businesses in Ireland. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us:

  • Email: info@taxbridge.ie
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +353 89 977 7898

2. What we collect

We only collect the information we need to provide our services and meet our legal obligations. Depending on how you deal with us, this may include:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number and postal address.
  • Identity details: your PPS number, date of birth, and copies of photo ID and proof of address (required for anti-money laundering checks).
  • Tax and financial information: employment and income details, P60s and employment detail summaries, Revenue correspondence, receipts for medical, rent and other expenses, bank details for refunds, and business records.
  • Family and household details: where relevant to your tax affairs, such as a spouse or civil partner for joint assessment, or dependants for certain credits.
  • Business information: company and VAT numbers, accounts, payroll and employee data you share with us to provide bookkeeping or payroll services.
  • Messages: the content of enquiries, questions and reviews you send us through our website, WhatsApp, email or phone.

Some of this information, such as health-related medical expense receipts, may reveal special category data. We only process it where needed to claim a relief on your behalf, and with appropriate safeguards.

4. Website forms & WhatsApp

When you submit a form on our website, your browser opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled in a message to us. Nothing is sent until you press Send yourself. Once sent, your message is delivered through WhatsApp, a service provided by WhatsApp Ireland Limited (Meta), and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. WhatsApp messages are end-to-end encrypted.

If you would prefer not to use WhatsApp, you can email us at info@taxbridge.ie or call us instead.

Please don't send sensitive documents by WhatsApp

For your security, please don't send PPS numbers, bank details or tax documents in your first message. We will arrange a secure way to collect these once you become a client.

5. Who we share data with

We only share your personal data where necessary, and only with:

  • The Revenue Commissioners, when we file returns, claims and registrations as your tax agent.
  • The Companies Registration Office (CRO) and the Register of Beneficial Ownership, for company formations and filings.
  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as accounting and payroll software, secure cloud storage and email providers. They act on our instructions under data processing agreements.
  • Professional advisers, such as our insurers, auditors or legal advisers, where needed.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where we are legally required to, for example under anti-money laundering law.

Where any provider stores data outside the European Economic Area, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Tax and accounting records: at least six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, in line with Revenue's record-keeping requirements.
  • Anti-money laundering records: at least five years after our business relationship with you ends, as required by law.
  • Enquiries from people who do not become clients: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

After these periods, we securely delete or destroy your data.

7. How we protect your data

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls, password-protected payslips and documents, encrypted storage and secure file transfer. Only people who need your data to do their job can access it, and they are bound by confidentiality.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Erase your data, where we are not legally required to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to how we process your data.
  • Data portability, to receive your data in a commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@taxbridge.ie. We will respond within one month, and there is normally no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, or at www.dataprotection.ie. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

9. Cookies & browser storage

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's local storage only to remember your light or dark mode choice, your article votes, and any reviews or questions you submit on the site. This information stays on your own device.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare). These providers may receive your IP address when the page loads, as is necessary to deliver the files to your browser.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the law changes. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

This page is provided for general information about how TaxBridge works. If you have any questions, contact us at info@taxbridge.ie.
